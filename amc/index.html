AMC & Service Contracts

Keep your systems running with a maintenance plan that fits how you actually use them.

Annual Maintenance Contract – Comprehensive (AMC)

We assess your full specification and fix one annual amount that covers spare parts. This includes preventive maintenance at least once a month, plus service as needed through the year.

Annual Service Contract – Non-Comprehensive (ASC)

Our engineers service all model configurations for a fixed reasonable amount, excluding spare parts cost. Covers preventive maintenance and on-demand service through the contract period.

On-Call Services

Experienced service engineers respond to requests within working hours when you don't need a full annual contract.
